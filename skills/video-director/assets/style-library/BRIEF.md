# Image brief: animation style library

Use your built-in image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Make one image per style below and copy each PNG to the exact file name given, in this folder. The library's pictures are then cropped to 16:9 and saved as 768x432 WebP (quality 72) under the same names. These are example pictures for a library of animation styles, so a person can compare styles side by side. The images show THE SAME simple scene, drawn in a different style each time:

**The scene (no people):** a small wooden desk by a window. On it: an open laptop whose screen shows a simple picture of green hills under a blue sky, a potted plant with broad leaves, an angled desk lamp that is switched on, a mug and a closed notebook. Seen from a three-quarter front angle.

**The creature** (only where a line below asks for it): a small, round, friendly made-up creature about the size of the mug, with a soft round body, two dot eyes, a small smile and stubby arms, sitting on the desk and waving at the laptop. It is not an animal, a person or any existing character.

**People** appear only where a line below describes one. Show them naturally and respectfully: real proportions, nothing exaggerated or made comic about their face or body.

**Rules for every image**
- Landscape, 3:2 (1536x1024). Keep the desk and everything on it in the middle of the frame with space around it, so the picture can be cropped to 16:9 without losing anything.
- The style must be obvious at a glance, even as a small thumbnail. Push the style; don't make a plain photo with a light filter.
- No readable text anywhere: no words, letters or numbers on screens, papers, signs or interface. Where interface text would be, use blank bars. The only exception is an image whose line below says otherwise.
- No logos or brand marks (the laptop lid is plain), no watermarks, no signature.
- No real or famous people. Don't copy any specific film, show, game, character or artist's work; make an original picture in the style described.
- One picture per file: no grids of variations, no borders, unless the line says so.
- Look at each image after you make it. If the style isn't clear, or there is stray text, make it again before moving on.

**The images**

### Drawn and illustrated

1. `flat-vector.png` — Clean flat vector illustration: simple geometric shapes, bold flat colors, no outlines, subtle flat shadows; the desk, lamp, plant and window as simple shapes.
2. `soft-rounded-characters.png` — Soft rounded illustration with the creature sitting on the desk waving at the laptop: blobby simplified shapes, warm orange, peach and soft yellow palette, no hard edges anywhere, calm and reassuring.
3. `whiteboard.png` — Black marker line drawing of the desk on a clean white whiteboard: simple confident lines, minimal detail. A real hand holding a black marker is finishing the last line of the lamp.
4. `pencil-sketch.png` — Graphite pencil sketch of the desk on white paper: visible hatching and strokes, a few light construction lines, edges left unfinished, gray tones only.
5. `rotoscope.png` — Looks traced from real footage: a Latina woman in her 40s with her hair tied back stands beside the desk opening the window, with realistic proportions and pose, but everything drawn as flat areas of color with smooth even outlines, a slightly dreamy palette of teal, violet and amber.
6. `watercolour.png` — Watercolour painting of the desk on white textured paper: soft-edged washes, pigment blooms where colors meet, loose edges, faint pencil underdrawing showing through.
7. `painterly-oil.png` — Oil painting of the desk with thick visible impasto brushstrokes; every surface built from swirling, directional strokes of rich color, post-impressionist energy.
8. `comic-book.png` — Comic book panel of the desk: bold black ink outlines, halftone dot shading, flat printed colors slightly offset from the lines like a misregistered print, a dramatic angle, action lines bursting from the switched-on lamp, a thin panel border. No speech bubbles or lettering.
9. `anime-cel.png` — Japanese hand-drawn animation look: the desk drawn with clean thin outlines and flat cel shading with one shadow tone, in front of a richly painted background of the window, a bright sky with big clouds and sunlit plants.
10. `mid-century-graphic.png` — 1950s modern graphic illustration of the desk: flat geometric shapes, a limited palette of mustard, teal, coral and black, shapes slightly offset from their outlines, light paper texture.
11. `silhouette.png` — Black cut-paper silhouette in profile: a person seen only as a black silhouette leans over the desk to water the plant with a small watering can; the desk, the lamp, the laptop and the plant are intricate black cut-out shapes too, against a glowing background of warm orange fading to deep blue light. No face details.
12. `psychedelic.png` — 1960s psychedelic poster look: saturated wavy bands of orange, hot pink, purple and lime flowing around the desk, lamp and plant, bold outlines, surreal swirling shapes, no lettering.
13. `hand-drawn-over-3d.png` — Looks like a 3D animated film frame of the desk (rounded volumes, soft lighting, depth of field, a camera angle slightly from below) redrawn as clean black hand-drawn ink lines with flat soft gray tones, almost entirely black, white and gray. A single accent of soft red: the mug only. Clearly a drawing with the depth of a 3D scene, not a pencil sketch.

### Paper, craft and physical

1. `clay-stop-motion.png` — Modeled plasticine set photographed as stop motion: the desk, laptop, lamp, mug and plant all made of clay, with the creature in clay sitting on the desk waving at the laptop. Visible thumbprints and tool marks, slightly lumpy shapes, miniature set lighting, shallow depth of field.
2. `puppet-stop-motion.png` — The creature as a detailed handmade felt-and-fabric puppet on a miniature set: a tiny real wooden desk, laptop, lamp and plant, warm practical lighting, a slightly uncanny storybook mood.
3. `object-stop-motion.png` — Real close-up photo of a tabletop scene built from everyday objects: the desk is a wooden ruler on two erasers, the laptop is an open matchbox with a tiny picture of hills inside, the lamp is a bent paperclip with a bottle-cap shade, the plant is a sprig of rosemary in a thimble, the mug is a button.
4. `pixilation.png` — Real photograph of a real person shot as stop motion: a Black man in his 30s in a blue shirt sits on the desk chair, which hovers a few inches off the floor as if gliding across the room on its own, his body perfectly still and upright. A faint ghost of his previous position is visible beside him, like a frame left behind.
5. `cut-paper.png` — Flat cut-paper animation photographed from above on a dark green table: the angled desk lamp is made of separate flat paper pieces (base, two arms, shade) joined by visible round brass paper fasteners at each joint, so it is clearly a jointed paper puppet. The desk, laptop, mug and plant are flat paper cut-outs too, with old engraving-style printed textures on some pieces and small drop shadows under each layer.
6. `collage.png` — Layered collage: torn newspaper (blurred, unreadable print), patterned papers and photo fragments overlap to form the desk, laptop, lamp, plant and window, with torn edges and glue shadows.
7. `origami.png` — The whole scene folded from paper: angular faceted folded-paper desk, laptop, lamp, mug and plant in white and a few solid colors, crisp creases, soft studio light.
8. `sand.png` — Sand animation, photographed from straight above: a glass light table glowing bright white from below; the desk scene is drawn in a thin layer of dark brown-black sand, so the light shines through wherever the sand is thin or wiped away. Lines are made by wiping sand with fingertips: soft grainy edges, individual grains visible, one fingertip resting at the edge of the frame. Only two tones: dark sand and glowing light. No color, no paper texture.

### 3D and texture

1. `character-3d.png` — Rounded, appealing 3D animated-film look: the creature as a lit 3D character sitting on the desk waving at the laptop, soft lighting, smooth materials, expressive dot eyes, a warm cozy set.
2. `painterly-3d.png` — A 3D animated-film frame of the desk with chunky stylized sculpted forms (a blocky desk, a heavy angular lamp, an oversized mug) and hand-painted textures: visible brushstrokes painted onto the 3D surfaces, painted rim light, dramatic lighting with teal shadows and warm orange highlights. It must read as 3D models that were painted, not a flat oil painting.
3. `skeuomorphic.png` — On the desk, instead of a plain laptop, a chunky physical-looking machine: brushed metal and leather texture, a round analog needle meter, real toggle switches and knobs, and a glass screen showing the hills. Realistic textures and shadows.
4. `neumorphism.png` — A pale light-gray interface filling the frame: a soft raised rounded panel showing a picture of the desk by the window, and round soft raised and pressed-in buttons with simple icons (play, light bulb, plant leaf), soft extruded shadows. Keep the icons dark enough to see clearly.
5. `topographic.png` — Drawn as a topographic map on white paper: the desk, the laptop, the lamp and the plant are made only of concentric contour lines that follow their shapes like elevation rings, with a dotted route line from the lamp to the laptop and small triangle markers. Dark green, brown and black lines on white. No numbers.
6. `flow-field.png` — Generative flow-field art: the whole frame, edge to edge, is filled with thousands of fine thin colored lines (teal, magenta, gold, white) on a dark navy ground, all flowing in smooth curving currents like wind or water. The desk, the lamp, the laptop and the plant appear only because the lines bend and bunch along their outlines; nothing is drawn as a solid object or painted surface. The lines are the whole picture.
7. `spirograph.png` — Fine overlapping looping lines forming radial spirograph rosettes; the desk, the lamp, the laptop and the plant are built from these loops in a few colors on white.

To add a style: write its line in the same form under its group, make the image with Codex as `references/media.md` describes, look at it, crop and convert it as above, link it in `references/video-styles.md`, and rebuild `index.html` with `scripts/build_style_library.py`.
